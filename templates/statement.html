📝 My Professional Statement

A look into my engineering mindset, problem-solving approach, and technical journey.

Welcome to my portfolio! As a Systems and Technical Support Engineer, I believe that a great engineer does more than just fix technical problems when they appear - they build systems that prevent those problems from happening in the first place. Throughout my career, I have found my greatest fulfillment in diving into complex system anomalies, investigating root causes, and creating smart solutions to streamline operations.

During my time at Antares Vision Group, I have regularly provided advanced technical support for critical industrial deployments. By diving deep into system logs using Elasticsearch and tracking data states via MySQL, I learned exactly how enterprise software environments behave under stress. However, I quickly realized that identifying an infrastructure bottleneck manually is only half the battle. This realization drove me to actively expand my skill set into advanced systems administration and scripting frameworks - culminating in my professional certifications in Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL), Microsoft SQL Server, and completing the intensive 100 Days of Code™ Python Bootcamp.

I focus heavily on combining technical empathy and remote support skills with deep system diagnostics. Using Python, Windows Batch, and Linux Bash scripts, I enjoy building custom utilities designed to automatically parse logs, monitor environment health, and eliminate repetitive incidents entirely. I bring a robust engineering combination to my work: an advanced academic foundation (M.Sc. from Politecnico di Torino), hands-on deployment and technical support experience, and the scripting logic required to keep modern enterprise systems running reliably.

I am always excited to connect with fellow engineers, tech enthusiasts, and forward-thinking teams. Thank you for taking the time to explore my digital profile, and feel free to reach out if you would like to connect!

Best regards,
